**Goal:** a school publishes workshops and intensives as events with their own price, sign-up,
waiting list and receipt. Members register themselves and their dependents on a public events page.
The office runs events in the web, and an event's registrants show up wherever a lesson's
participants do: announcements, feeds, statistics, participant lists, the data export.

**Architecture:**

- **Already built:**
  - the domain (`internal/domain/event.go`) and both stores (`Events()`, `EventRegistrations()`,
    migration 00031, contract cases in `internal/store/storetest/events.go`);
  - the office's service and API (`service.EventService` in `service/events.go`,
    `event_registrations.go`, `event_payment.go`; `httpapi/event_admin.go`, migration 00032);
  - the event fee receipt (`ReceiptService.issueEventFee`, Storno through `storno`).
- **Sessions are lessons:** they are `workshop` lessons with `Lesson.EventID`. Booking one is a 409
  `event-session`; a registration is for the whole event.
- **This plan adds:**
  - the member and public API, with mails and the event waiting list;
  - the integration with announcements, feeds, statistics, participants and privacy;
  - the demo seed;
  - the web: office, public and member pages, and the Programme and Werkstatt slots;
  - the `events` commands.

**Tech stack:** Go (chi, Huma v2), PostgreSQL and the memory store, React TS (TanStack Router and
Query, shadcn/ui, react-hook-form with zod, react-i18next), Playwright.

**Spec:** [ADR 0019](../../decisions/0019-events-and-workshops.md) and PLAN.md Phase 10. The user's
decisions for this plan (2026-11-03) are in "Decisions" below.

## Decisions

These were taken with the user on 2026-11-03. Each owning item records the decision as an ADR 0019
amendment.

1. **An invite that runs out cancels the registration.** It becomes `canceled`, with the mail
   `event-invite-expired`; the person may join again at the end of the list. The freed place goes to
   the next person waiting. There is no new status and no "stay on the list". Owner: 2.1.
2. **My events shows the family's registrations with their receipts.** A guardian may download the
   receipt and Storno PDFs of a registration of their own person or a dependent, whoever the payer
   is. Access goes by registration, not by payer. Owner: 1.1.
3. **Anonymizing a person cancels their open registrations** for events whose first scheduled
   session has not started. The places go to the waiting list. Payments are kept: there is no
   automatic refund, and the receipts stay (ADR 0016). Owner: 2.3.
4. **A session's participant list shows the event's registered persons and guests**, read-only:
   they are not bookings, and they cannot be removed, marked attended or moved there. Owner: 2.2.
5. **Lock order** (recorded by 1.1 in architecture.md and the ADR): event → persons → term →
   class → lesson → passes. `AddRegistration` already locks the event before the person, so a write
   that starts from a person (anonymize, merge) reads that person's open registrations first, locks
   their events in ID order, and only then locks the persons.
6. **Statistics** (owner 2.2):
   - event fees are a total of their own, next to the pass sales, which no longer include them;
   - sessions are left out of booking utilization, as rentals are;
   - a new "events" section lists each event in the range with its sign-ups and fee revenue.

## Review focus

These are the inputs and conditions most likely to bite. Each has its test in the owning task.

1. **The last place, twice at once.** Two members register different persons for an event's last
   place concurrently. One gets it and the other gets 409 `full`; the counts stay within the
   capacity. On both stores (`servicetest`). Owner: 1.1.
2. **Drafts and canceled events stay private.** A draft is absent from `listEvents`, and `getEvent`
   with its slug and the public image are a 404 (also for a signed-in member). A canceled event is
   absent from `listEvents`; its page says canceled and takes no registration. Owner: 1.1.
3. **Another family is a 404.** Registering, cancelling, the My events list and a receipt PDF for a
   person who is neither the caller nor their dependent all answer 404, never 403. Owner: 1.1.
4. **An invite link works once.** Accepting twice finds nothing the second time. An expired link
   finds nothing, and the freed place moves to the next person exactly once, whichever runs first:
   the job or the second accept. Owner: 2.1.
5. **Anonymized and merged persons.** An anonymized registrant shows as `anonymized` (no former
   name) in the registration lists and the participant list. A merged person's registrations follow
   the survivor, and both stores agree. Owner: 2.3, with the list views in 2.2.
